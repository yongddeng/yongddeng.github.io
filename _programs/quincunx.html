Each peg sends a ball right with probability p, so its bin is X ~ Bin(n, p). Dn = supz |P(Zn ≤ z) − Φ(z)| comes from the binomial itself, not the balls. The bound is C(p² + q²)/√npq, with C = 0.4748 for i.i.d. summands (Shevtsova, 2011); the 0.56 in (#6) is for summands not identically distributed.

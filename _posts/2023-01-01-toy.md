<div class="exe-notes" hidden>
<p data-note="cubes">One Mixtral decoder layer as tensors of cubes, top to bottom: x, the attention tensors and caches, h, the router and the experts, y, and the LM head. Scrolling this pane, or Run, moves one token’s row down through them: every fold, drop and merge plays forward on the way down and backward on the way up, and every weight is drawn, lit by input × weight as the token crosses it.</p>
<p data-note="maths">The maths of the step the token is at, a section a component: what each assumes, folded to one line, then its line worked step by step, by hand and in PyTorch, in the numbers of the token on the left.</p>
<p data-note="tokens">The sentence’s six tokens, which Run takes through the layer one after another; a click runs another, its numbers on both sides.</p>
<p data-note="views">The cubes face on, or turned to show their depth.</p>
